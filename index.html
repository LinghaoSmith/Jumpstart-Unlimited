Changelog

This project originally started in November of 2022 as Jumpstart Extended, a workshop page for Tabletop Simulator. Due to poor management on my part, the image backs that were on all the cards in on that program broke since I used Discord as an image hosting service. The project has been abandoned since September 2024 because of that. Until now...
